# AI-Powered SEO Content Production: Playbook & SOP

## 1. Core Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Step 1: Define the Prompts and Journeys**
Define the prompts and journeys that matter by building a prompt library from real buyer language, such as sales transcripts and support logs, rather than relying on traditional keyword tools (source: Aleyda Solis, [https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/) from 27.05.2026). Ensure you are tracking prompt coverage, recommendation rates, and comparative win rates separately per platform (source: Aleyda Solis, [https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/) from 27.05.2026).

**Step 2: Engineer Semantic EAV Architecture**
Create Topical Maps and ensure Entity-Attribute-Value (EAV) coverage systematically before generating any content (source: Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR, [https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a](https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a) from 29.06.2024). Assign one macro context per page to maintain a single, clear semantic focus (source: Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR, [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mSHq_HxOyTA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mSHq_HxOyTA) from 05.02.2026).

**Step 3: Passage-Level Content Generation**
Format decision-support content with question-format H2 headings paired with approximately 40-word extractive answers (source: Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR, [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mSHq_HxOyTA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mSHq_HxOyTA) from 05.02.2026). Focus on passage-level relevance and retrieval precision, optimizing for the synthetic queries generated by AI systems (source: Mike King, [https://ipullrank.com/content-relevance](https://ipullrank.com/content-relevance) from 20.04.2023). Structure the content to be retrievable and extractable, ensuring individual sections can stand alone as distinct "chunks" (source: Aleyda Solis, [https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/) from 27.05.2026).

**Step 4: Human-in-the-Loop Verification**
Implement a rigorous human verification step, as the cost to automate content is falling, but human cognition to verify it remains the main bottleneck (source: Kevin Indig, [https://www.growth-memo.com/p/ai-changed-my-work-and-yours-too](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/ai-changed-my-work-and-yours-too) from 04.05.2026).

## 2. Where experts disagree

**Example 1: The Value of Traditional Keyword Tools vs. Buyer Language**

* **What Nathan Gotch recommends:** Utilizing standard keyword research and traditional SEO processes to build AI SaaS and content strategies (source: Nathan Gotch, [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y88G1JbGsEE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y88G1JbGsEE) from 26.03.2026).
* **What Aleyda Solis recommends:** Defining prompts directly from real buyer language like sales transcripts and support logs, skipping keyword tools entirely (source: Aleyda Solis, [https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/) from 27.05.2026).


* **Which side I take and why:** I side with Aleyda Solis. AI chatbots answer specific situational queries better than exact-match volume keywords, so raw buyer language is a more accurate data source for content targeting.



**Example 2: AI Scaling vs. The Verification Gap**

* **What Tomasz Niezgoda recommends:** Scaling content and product marketing rapidly using SaaS SEO tools to drive massive growth (source: Tomasz Niezgoda, [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_huJv4Xp6E](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_huJv4Xp6E) from 06.08.2024).
* **What Kevin Indig recommends:** Acknowledging that while AI speeds up production 3-10x, it must not come at the cost of trust, meaning human judgment is the ultimate bottleneck for verification (source: Kevin Indig, [https://www.growth-memo.com/p/ai-changed-my-work-and-yours-too](https://www.growth-memo.com/p/ai-changed-my-work-and-yours-too) from 04.05.2026).


* **Which side I take and why:** I side with Kevin Indig. AI scale without strict human editorial verification destroys user trust, rendering any traffic gains useless.



**Example 3: Basic NLP Tools vs. Deep Semantic Agents**

* **What generic SEO practitioners recommend:** Using simple named-entity recognition (NER) and lemmatization tools to score AI content (source: Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR, [https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a](https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a) from 29.06.2024).
* **What Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR recommends:** Building Semantic Content Networks using custom agents that encode deep Entity-Attribute-Value (EAV) modeling rather than shallow NER (source: Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR, [https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a](https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a) from 29.06.2024).
* **Which side I take and why:** I side with Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR. Surface-level keyword insertion creates easily identifiable, low-quality AI footprints, whereas deep semantic alignment legitimately satisfies retrieval engines.

## 3. What I rejected and why

1. **Gaming AI Systems:** I rejected the idea of manufacturing community signals, fabricating reviews, or attempting prompt injection to boost visibility (source: Aleyda Solis, [https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/](https://www.aleydasolis.com/en/ai-search/ai-search-optimization-checklist/) from 27.05.2026). **Reasoning:** As Aleyda Solis points out, these exploits trade durable, long-term trust for short-term visibility, and they get patched quickly.


2. **Basic NER (Named-Entity Recognition) Tools:** I rejected using basic out-of-the-box SEO content tools that only perform lemmatization or shallow NER (source: Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR, [https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a](https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a) from 29.06.2024). **Reasoning:** Search engine NLP systems parse relational meaning via predicates, meaning shallow keyword counting does not actually build Topical Authority.

## 4. My original ideas

**Self-Healing LangGraph SEO Pipelines for Automated Pre-Verification**
Rather than following a linear generation path (Prompt → Draft → Human Editor), I propose building a functional, multi-agent AI framework using LangGraph to evaluate content against Entity-Attribute-Value models prior to human review.

**Why it could work:**
By setting up an iterative, graph-based logic flow, one agent drafts the passage, while another dedicated "Retrieval Evaluator" agent automatically checks it for synthetic query coverage and semantic gaps. If the chunk fails the evaluation, the agent loop self-heals by rewriting the specific passage before it ever reaches the human editor. This drastically reduces the manual human verification bottleneck described by Kevin Indig, filtering factual and semantic errors programmatically at scale.

---

## 5. Weaknesses of this playbook

* **Human Bottleneck Constraints:** Even with advanced agents assisting in production, the ultimate bottleneck remains human cognitive load for verification. The playbook assumes a team has enough subject matter experts to handle the final review without simply rubber-stamping the AI's output.


* **Algorithmic Shift Risks:** Relying heavily on passage-level chunking and synthetic query optimization assumes AI chatbots (GPTBot, PerplexityBot) will continue extracting data this way. If retrieval architectures shift radically, the formatting rules will need immediate updating.


* **Complex Technical Overhead:** Building out proper Entity-Attribute-Value models and custom agentic frameworks requires significant engineering overhead compared to standard content marketing workflows.

## 6. Who I would NOT recommend following and why

I would not recommend following **Tomasz Niezgoda (Surfer)** (source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_huJv4Xp6E from 06.08.2024).

**Why:** Traditional "Product-led" SEO SaaS tools heavily promote quantitative correlation, essentially counting keyword frequency and enforcing basic NLP entity scores. Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR explicitly warns against this shallow approach of treating SEO as simple lemmatization rather than actual semantic relationship building. Furthermore, relying on standard optimization tool metrics creates the exact "verification gap" Kevin Indig warns about, where content is scaled faster than its actual quality and trust can be measured. Treating content as an algorithmic checklist rather than a deep semantic system is a fragile long-term strategy.
